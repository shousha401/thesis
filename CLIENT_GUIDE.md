# Running the site

A guide for the three of you. No technical knowledge needed — if you can post
on Instagram, you can do all of this.

Everything happens at **yourdomain.com/admin**, and it all works on your phone.

> **The one rule worth knowing:** when you save something, it is live on the
> site straight away. There is no "publish the website" step and nobody needs to
> deploy anything. If you tick *Publish* and hit Save, it is public.

## 1. Logging in

There is no password to remember.

1. Go to **yourdomain.com/admin**.
2. Type your email address and tap **Email me a link**.
3. You will see a "Check your email" screen. **The email can take up to a
   minute** — that is normal.
4. Open the email and tap the link. You are in.

_[screenshot: the login screen]_
_[screenshot: the "check your email" screen]_

**If the email doesn't arrive:**

- Check your spam or promotions folder.
- Wait for the **Resend the link** button to become tappable (it counts down
  from 60 seconds), then tap it.
- Make sure you typed the same address the site knows about. If you use a
  different email, you will get a polite "this account can't get in" page —
  that is the site working correctly, not a bug.

Each link works once and expires after an hour. If you tap an old link, you'll
be sent back to the login screen with a note explaining why.

## 2. Adding an episode

This is the main thing you'll do. It takes about a minute.

1. Tap **Add a new episode** on the dashboard (or **Episodes → New episode**).
2. **Video link** — paste the link to the episode. Anything works:
   - the YouTube address bar link
   - the YouTube **Share** button link
   - a Shorts, live, or `youtu.be` link
   - an Instagram Reel or a TikTok link

   _[screenshot: pasting a link on a phone]_

3. **Title** — what the episode is called.
4. **Description** — write as much as you like. Blank lines between paragraphs
   are kept exactly as you type them.
5. **Episode number** is filled in for you — change it if it's wrong. **Season**
   can be left blank.
6. **Publish date** — leave blank and today's date is used.
7. **Thumbnail** — for YouTube, skip this: the site uses YouTube's own picture
   automatically. For Instagram and TikTok you **must** upload one, because
   those apps don't give us a picture.
   - Whenever you upload a picture, you also have to fill in **Image
     description**. One short sentence describing the photo. This is what
     someone using a screen reader hears, so it isn't optional.
8. **Listen links** — optional. Paste the Spotify and Apple links for this
   episode if you have them.
9. Tick **Publish this episode**.
10. Tap **Save episode**.

_[screenshot: the filled-in form with the Save button]_

You'll land back on the episode list with "Saved. The site is already updated."
The episode is now on the site — on the home page, on the Episodes page, and on
its own page.

**Editing later:** tap **Edit** on any episode. You can change anything,
including the title. The web address stays the same on purpose, so links you've
already shared on Instagram keep working.

**Taking something down:** tap **Unpublish**. It disappears from the site
immediately but stays in your list so you can put it back.

## 3. Adding a clip

Same idea, shorter form. Clips are the Shorts, Reels and TikToks.

1. **Clips → New clip**.
2. Paste the link to the Short, Reel or TikTok.
3. **Title** and a one-line **caption**.
4. **From which episode?** — pick the episode the clip came from, if it came
   from one. This adds a "watch the full episode" link on the clip's page.
   Leave it as *Not from an episode* for standalone clips.
5. **Thumbnail** — same rule: YouTube is automatic, Instagram and TikTok need an
   upload plus an image description.
6. Tick **Publish this clip** and tap **Save clip**.

_[screenshot: the clip form with the episode picker open]_

Every clip gets its own page, which is what makes them findable on Google and
shareable on their own.

## 4. Setting up a live stream

### Before the stream

1. **Live → New event**.
2. **Title** — what you're calling it.
3. **Where is it?** — YouTube, Instagram, TikTok or somewhere else.
4. **Link** — where people should go to watch.
5. **Date and time** — in your own local time.
6. Leave **Status** as *Scheduled*.
7. **Save event**.

A banner now appears at the top of the home page with the date, and the Live
page shows the details.

_[screenshot: the upcoming-live banner on the home page]_

### When you go on air

Open **Live** and tap **Go live now**. That's it — one tap.

- The home banner changes to **Live now**.
- If you're on YouTube, the stream plays right on the Live page.
- If you're on Instagram, the Live page shows a "We're live on Instagram — tap
  to join" button instead, because Instagram doesn't let other websites play
  their streams. This is expected.

_[screenshot: the Live page while on air]_

### Afterwards

Tap **End the stream**. Then tap **Edit** and paste a **Replay link** so people
who missed it can still watch. The Live page will show the replay whenever
nothing else is scheduled.

## 5. Editing your bio

1. **Hosts** → tap **Edit** next to your name.
2. Change your **name**, **star sign**, and **bio**. Line breaks are kept.
3. **Photo** — upload one, and fill in the **photo description** (one sentence
   describing the picture, for screen readers). Without a photo, the site shows
   your initial in a nice typeface, so there's no rush.
4. **Your links** — Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn. Leave any blank to hide it.
5. **Save changes**.

_[screenshot: the host edit form]_

**About the "web address" box:** this is the end of your personal page's link
(`/hosts/your-name`). Right now it's a placeholder. Change it once, when you
settle on how you want your name to appear — after that, anyone who has shared
your old link will find a "page not found", so it's worth doing early and then
leaving alone.

### Changing the words on the About page

**Settings → Links & about**. Here you can edit:

- **Where to listen** — the YouTube, Spotify and Apple links in the header and
  footer of every page.
- **Social links** and a contact email.
- **How we met** — shown on the Hosts page.
- **About the show** — the whole About page. Leave a blank line between
  paragraphs; the first paragraph is styled larger, like a magazine intro.

## 6. When something goes wrong

**"Nothing was saved. Please fix the highlighted fields."**
Something in the form needs attention. The problem field is marked in pink with
a short explanation. Nothing you typed is lost — fix it and save again.

**A message about the video link.**
The site checks the link before saving, so a broken link can't quietly end up on
the site. The message tells you what to do — usually "open the video and copy
the link from the address bar".

**"TikTok does not give us a thumbnail, so please upload one."**
Exactly what it says. TikTok and Instagram don't share their preview pictures,
so pick a picture yourself. A screenshot from the clip is fine.

**You deleted something by accident.**
Nothing is really gone. On the Episodes or Clips list, tap **View deleted
episodes** at the top, then **Restore**.

**You can't get in.**
- Wrong email → you'll see "This account can't get in". Try the address the site
  knows.
- No email arriving → check spam, then use **Resend the link**.
- Still stuck → whoever set the site up can add or fix your email address in
  about thirty seconds.

**Something looks wrong on the site itself.**
Changes appear immediately, so if you saved something and don't see it, first
pull down to refresh the page. If it's still wrong, take a screenshot and send
it on — don't try to fix it by deleting and re-adding.
